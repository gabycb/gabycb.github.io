# About

I am a technology leader with experience at Microsoft, NASA, MIT, and the Department of Defense. At MIT, I studied product design and machine learning for disaster response technology development, with some of my work published [here]((https://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/122221). In the military, I led cross-functional teams and analytical studies. Now in the tech world, I lead enterprise-level transformations for companies to become frontier and implement the latest in AI and agents at scale, follow my best practices and the latest on AI via my Medium page [@gabycbarrera](https://medium.com/@gabycbarrera). I work with various organizations for research on bias detection in algorithms and for outreach and community development in STEM programs.

My background spans military operations research, policy, applied machine learning, and enterprise AI architecture. Across these domains, my superpower is designing for human judgment under uncertainty.
